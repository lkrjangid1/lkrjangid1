## What it does

Ayuvo is a free health app for iPhone and Android that keeps nutrition, workouts, health records, medications and a local mirror of Apple Health or Health Connect in one place. There is no account and no Ayuvo server. Data is stored on the phone and leaves it only when the user asks, to a service they chose. The AI coach runs on the user's own API key from one of 15 providers, or on a model downloaded to the phone. The app is open source under the MIT licence, and its App Store and Google Play listings are coming soon.

## Features

- **Nutrition.** Log meals by photo (up to 10 at once), barcode through Open Food Facts, voice or text, and review calories, macros and 30+ nutrients before saving.
- **Workouts.** GPS walks, runs, rides and hikes with Lock Screen, widget and Apple Watch controls, plus a set-and-rep diary with a 1,300+ exercise library.
- **Health data hub.** Every Apple Health or Health Connect type the user grants, mirrored into a local database with day-to-year charts.
- **Records and medications.** Scanned reports are read on the phone, next to a medications list.
- **Insights.** Morning Recovery, Ayuvo Health Age, a Daily Review and 60 derived metrics, such as resting heart rate, heart-rate zones, sleep regularity and cardio fitness.
- **AI coach.** Multi-turn chat about the user's own diary, workouts and trends. Health data is shared only behind a visible consent toggle.
- **On-device AI.** Gemma, Qwen and MedGemma models that run on the phone, for users who don't want to use an API key.
- **Fasting and water.** Optional timers and goals, off by default.
- **Platform extras.** An Apple Watch app, widgets, 47 Siri and Shortcuts actions, 18 languages, 18 accent colours, and Export All Data to move between iPhone and Android.

## How it's built

Ayuvo is two native apps: SwiftUI on iPhone, with a watch app, widgets and a share extension, and Kotlin with Jetpack Compose on Android. A `shared/` folder holds what both apps read: the exercise dataset, plus the health metric registry and SQLite schema that define how Apple Health and Health Connect data is mirrored. API keys are kept in the iOS Keychain and in Android EncryptedSharedPreferences. The website is a static site generated by a Python build script and hosted on Firebase.

Ayuvo started from Fud AI, an MIT-licensed calorie tracker by Apoorv Darshan. Its copyright notice is kept in the licence and shown in the apps under Settings → Legal → Licenses.

## What I'd point out

- **No first-party servers.** No accounts, and no analytics, crash or ad SDKs. Network calls go only to services the user picked, such as their AI provider, Open Food Facts for barcodes and Hugging Face for model downloads.
- **Health data stays out of backups.** The health database and coach chat are excluded from OS backups and from the optional Google Drive backup on Android.
- **One data contract, two platforms.** The shared registry and schema keep health data in the same shape on both apps, so an export from one phone imports on the other.
