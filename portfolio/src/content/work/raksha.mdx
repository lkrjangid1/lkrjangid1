## What it does

Raksha ("protection") is a wearable band and a phone app. A double press on the band raises an SOS, and the app sends the wearer's live location to the emergency contacts set up in advance.

## Features

- **Double-press trigger.** The ESP32 band raises an alert with a two-press gesture, so the phone stays in the pocket.
- **Live location to contacts.** The SOS includes the wearer's current location and goes to the assigned contacts.
- **In-app SOS button.** The app's home screen has a large alert button as a second way to raise an SOS.
- **Emergency contacts.** A contacts tab where you add and manage the people who receive alerts.
- **Alert settings.** A menu on the home screen and a settings tab for customising how alerts behave.

## What I'd point out

- **Funding for a student prototype.** IIIT Surat's SSIP sanction order for "Raksha (A Women Safety Band)" is dated 18 December 2021. It covers prototype expenses of up to ₹50,000, reimbursed against bills under SSIP and Government of Gujarat rules.
- **Built by a three-person team.** The sanction letter names three team members.
