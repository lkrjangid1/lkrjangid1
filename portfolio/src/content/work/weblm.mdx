## What it does

Weblm is a chatbot for a single website. You give it a site, it extracts information from it, and you ask questions in conversation instead of clicking through pages. The aim is to make web-based data conversational and accessible.

## How it's built

It is written in Python, uses LangChain to connect the extracted site content to a language model, and uses ChatGPT to generate the answers.
