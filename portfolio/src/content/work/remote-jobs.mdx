## What it does

Remote Jobs helps people find remote work anywhere in the world. Instead of following dozens of job-board accounts and hashtags on X, users open one feed of recent remote-job posts, each shown with the posting account, the post text, its link preview and the date it was posted. The app is no longer listed on Google Play.

## Features

- **Job feed.** Remote-job posts from job boards and hiring accounts on X, shown as cards with the original text, links and post date.
- **Search by role.** A search box with role suggestions such as software developer, full stack, frontend, backend, iOS, Android, Node.js and React.
- **Job-type chips.** Quick filters for freelance, part-time, full-time and internship roles.
- **Bookmarks.** Save a job with one tap and find it later in a separate bookmarks screen.
- **Share and open.** Share a post, or open its apply link in the browser.
- **Paged browsing.** Previous and next page controls for moving through older posts.
- **Dark mode.** A dark theme toggle in the side menu, next to About, Share and Feedback.

## How it's built

The app is written in Flutter for Android, with Firebase behind it. The data side is an automated pipeline that retrieves remote-job posts from X (Twitter) and publishes them to the feeds the app reads, so new openings appear without anyone curating them by hand. Users see a post's original wording and links rather than a rewritten summary.

## What I'd point out

- **Source where the jobs are posted.** Pulling straight from X means the feed fills itself from the accounts that announce openings, with no manual curation step.
- **Small, focused scope.** One feed, role search, type filters and bookmarks, which was enough to reach more than 12,000 users.
