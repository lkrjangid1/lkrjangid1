## What it does

FinSight AI is an AI-driven finance companion I built at Univest. It explains a stock's fundamentals and technicals, helps you build an options strategy, and lets you place a trade in one click.

## Features

- **Stock insights.** AI-generated readings of a stock's fundamental and technical picture.
- **Options strategy builder.** Lets you assemble and inspect options strategies.
- **One-click trading.** Lets you act on an insight without leaving the app.
- **Broker-aware onboarding.** The app detects which broker apps are installed and tailors the journey and onboarding to match.

## What I'd point out

- **Two native codebases.** The same product was built in Kotlin for Android and in SwiftUI for iOS.
