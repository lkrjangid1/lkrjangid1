From a document
to a product maker.

The v1 site is fast and correct, but it reads like a résumé printed on beige. v2 keeps the data, static rendering and speed, and adds a face, kinetic type, real device mockups and a per-product colour system. Approve the look on the showcase page; the Astro port comes after.

  • Looping video over GIFs, lazy-loaded

Sources: Fueler, devplaybook, web.dev

Principles

Rules every section follows

Proof over adjectives

Every claim is a number with a link: Play Store, pub.dev, a FlutDev post or a case study.

Ten-second sections

Each block answers one question in a headline, a tagline and three chips. Details live one click deeper.

Motion explains, never blocks

Nothing waits on animation to be readable. Reduced motion gets the same page, still.

Questions and gaps

needed from Lokesh before the Astro port
  • Portrait

    Transparent cut-out at design/assets/portrait.webp, about 900×1200. The page shows a silhouette until then.

  • Screenshots

    Libgen, MBM, Raksha and Remote Jobs screenshots in projects.json are only ~117×260 px and look soft in frames. Re-export at 1080 px wide.

  • App icons

    Real icons (512 px PNG) for each product would replace the monogram squircles.

  • Video

    Optional 6–10 s screen recordings (Scalper chart, PushPatch rollout) as muted loops instead of cycling stills.

  • Testimonials

    Two or three real quotes from managers, clients or package users. Omitted until provided; nothing is invented.

  • Talks

    Any talks, GDG events or mentoring to add as a community strip?

  • Timeline

    Confirm the Timemarks title and dates, and when platform tooling started.

  • Dead links

    From projects.json: the Remote Jobs Play Store listing returns 404, and calculator.buckrate.com doesn't respond. Both tiles are unlinked until fixed.

  • Contact

    Public email for the CTA, and the résumé PDF to host at /resume.pdf (the Weebly copy is linked for now).
