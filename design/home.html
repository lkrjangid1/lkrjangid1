PushPatch

Fix production Flutter apps in minutes, not review cycles.

Dart-only patches to released apps, applied as binary diffs on device. Staged rollouts from 1%, live adoption and device-health monitoring, one-click rollback, and self-hosting. It grew out of the self-hosted code push I reverse-engineered from Shorebird at Univest, which saved ₹14L+ a year.

  • < 5 min fix to production
  • ₹14L+/yr saved at Univest
  • 1% staged rollouts

04 / 05Open source · Dart, JNI, libclang, JSI

native‑api‑bindgen

Use native APIs directly. No MethodChannel.

Reads the Android and Apple SDKs already on your machine and generates typed Dart and TypeScript bindings. A canonical IR feeds per-target generators that report a reason code for anything they skip.

bindgen1.80 ms
MethodChannel9.02 ms
